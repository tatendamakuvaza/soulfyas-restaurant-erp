### How This Book Came to Be

I completed a six-module data analytics course — SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, Stata and SPSS — and then faced the question every graduate faces: *what now?* The tools were in my hands, but the path from "I know some SQL and I can build a dashboard" to "I can run an analytics practice, teach a class, and ship a machine-learning system people trust" was nowhere written down.

This book is the answer, written in two volumes. The **foundation volume** (Parts I–VIII) builds the grammar: questions, data engineering, statistics, visualization, machine learning, big-data platforms, and the responsible operation of the whole. **This volume — the Extended Practitioner's Edition — begins where a solid foundations course ends.** It assumes you already carry a toolkit (any toolkit: the six modules above, a degree, a bootcamp, hard-won on-the-job training) and takes you the rest of the way: the special topics and the craft of teaching (Part IX), ten industry playbooks (Part X), the frontier methods that extend the working core (Part XI), and the delivery craft of the practising analyst (Part XII).

Every chapter is built on the same bridge: **From Your Toolkit** callouts connect each new idea back to the six foundation modules, because you never learn analytics by abandoning what you know — you learn it by extending it. And every concept lives in one running world: **Soulfya's Restaurant Group**, a fictional three-outlet restaurant chain in Harare whose data (generated by Appendix F, with every pattern planted and documented) carries the whole book — together with the ten companies of Part X's domain playbooks, who share its world.

### Who This Book Is For

- **Graduates of foundation courses** who know the tools and need the disciplines that turn tools into practice.
- **Working analysts** who want to move from reports to models, from models to systems, and from systems to delivered engagements.
- **Instructors** building the second semester or the honours year: this volume is sequenced as one (Chapters 49–80), with a slide kit (Appendix I), a case portfolio for seminars (Appendix J), and a complete instructor's guide in Chapter 50.
- **Self-taught practitioners** who want their advanced knowledge to have the same foundations a degree would have given them.

### How to Read This Book

Three habits, sustained across every chapter, are worth more than any tool in it:

1. **Ask the decision question before the data question.** Every chapter's first move is "what will someone do differently?"
2. **Name a baseline before praising a result.** Seasonal-naive, majority class, last year: if you cannot beat the cheapest alternative, use the alternative.
3. **Write for a reader who is not you.** Memos, model cards, logs: the artefact is the analysis.

Read with a lab open. Every chapter ends with a Practice Lab, and the labs are load-bearing: the prose is scaffolding for the labs, not the other way around. The data comes from Appendix F's generator — planted patterns, documented — or from the public datasets of Appendix E.

### The Appendices

Four appendices back this edition: **E** datasets and resources (public data, tools, and the reading list); **F** the Soulfya's practice-dataset generator (the script that fabricates the running case's universe, with every planted pattern documented); **I** the instructor slide kit (twelve starter decks, one per part); and **J** the extended case portfolio (five end-to-end cases with instructor numbers and traps). A companion reference section — Python and SQL primers, an algorithm quick-reference, a glossary, worked solutions, and the assessment bank — accompanies the foundation volume.

### A Note on the Running Case

Soulfya's Restaurant Group is fictional, and so is everyone in it — but nothing about its data is arbitrary. Its outlets (Avondale, Borrowdale, Bulawayo), its Soul Circle loyalty programme, its delivery channel and its reviews carry every pattern this book teaches: weekly seasonality, payday spikes, promo lifts with their selection biases, outlet differences, a genuine churn signal with a survival shape. Its world extends through Part X: the fashion house that stocks it, the telco that connects it, the logistics firm that delivers its cold chain, the water authority that supplies it — one fictional economy, planted and documented, from the first chapter to the last. If the chain feels real by Chapter 51, the book is working.
